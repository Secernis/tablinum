# Trademark and logo

The source code of Tablinum is licensed under the GNU General Public License
v3.0 (see `LICENSE`). **The name "Tablinum" and its logo are not covered by that
licence.**

This concerns:

- the mark, wordmark and lockups in `src/lib/brand/`
- the favicons and app icons in `public/` and `src-tauri/icons/`
- the name "Tablinum" as the designation of this application

## What that means in practice

You may fork this project, modify it and distribute it — that is what the GPL is
for. What you may not do is present your fork as "Tablinum" or ship it with this
logo. **Replace the brand with your own** before you distribute a fork.

The reason is not restriction for its own sake: a mark identifies who stands
behind a piece of software. If two different programs carry the same one, it
stops telling anyone anything — and bug reports for your fork end up with us.

The GPL itself takes the same view. Section 7(e) explicitly permits declining to
grant trademark rights, and it says so precisely because copyright licences and
trademarks answer different questions.

## What is explicitly allowed

- naming Tablinum in text, documentation, articles or talks
- showing the logo when writing about this project
- stating that your fork is *based on* Tablinum

## Questions

If you are unsure whether an intended use is covered, ask before you ship.
